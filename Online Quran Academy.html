FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which courses do you offer?

We offer Quran Translation, Hifz-ul-Quran, Quran Reading Lessons, Noorani Qaida, and Tajweed Lessons.

Which languages are lessons taught in?

All lessons are conducted in English or Urdu. Students can choose their preferred language.

Can beginners join?

Yes, eligible beginners can start with Noorani Qaida and progress toward Quran reading at their own pace.

Can children enroll?

Girls of all ages are welcome. Boys aged 15 and under are eligible to enroll. Boys over 15 are not eligible.

How does Hifz work?

Students follow a structured memorization and revision routine tailored to their individual ability and progress.

What is the difference between Qaida and Tajweed?

Qaida builds foundational Arabic reading skills, while Tajweed teaches the rules for correct Quranic recitation.
